The CurioSeek apex covers StudentOS, CreatorOS, LifeOS, and Fandom Vibes. Fandom Vibes is the broader fandom experience, with C-drama as its initial focus—not a separate “C-drama Wiki” product or an open-edit wiki. Its intended public scope includes C-drama reference/wiki materials, spoiler-aware drama/character/relationship guides, games/quizzes, curated Vibe Atlas collections, and content-engine experiences. Vibe Atlas is one experience within Fandom Vibes, not the whole world. Only verified, approved public content should be linked; label proposed additions and distinguish concept artwork from real public collections. Canonical-link approval and current public-content findings are recorded in replit.md and the public-content review, rather than inferred from older design mockups.

LifeOS is the proposed everyday operations and cross-domain coordination layer for people managing a multifaceted life. It spans email/calendar triage, receipts and finance, capture, and follow-through; Shibuya routes inputs to the right project or specialist workflow while each domain retains its depth and source of truth. The initial public concept can use a fictional workflow, such as a renovation email becoming a decision, budget consideration, and next action. Do not imply live integrations or expose private journals, personal records, or internal endpoints. No canonical destination is confirmed.

**Why:** The user clarified that Fandom Vibes is not a standalone wiki, C-drama is its initial rather than exclusive focus, and LifeOS connects everyday administration to specialist domains. Live editorial content can outgrow a concept mockup; the existence of a public reading does not make it spoiler-free. LifeOS has no confirmed destination.

**How to apply:** Keep all four areas represented when developing the apex. Use synthetic data for LifeOS demonstrations, verify public content before linking, and avoid launch claims or personal/internal data in either area.
