## Public versus proposed or private

- **Existing public:** the entry points above, including Vibe Atlas daily card drops and published editions. Calling the entire Vibe Atlas experience a “concept preview” is outdated.
- **Illustrative only:** the apex’s sun, moon, hills, and swatches are concept artwork, not actual released Vibe Atlas cards. Label them accordingly.
- **Not verified as released:** any additional named guide, game, collection, or future content-engine feature not evidenced here. Label new proposals as proposed and verify them separately before linking.
- **Not public editorial content:** personal saved collections, account records, internal tooling, private workspaces, and operator endpoints. Do not add apex links to them.
- The live site's broader membership, save, export, and account claims were not independently tested and are not promises made by the apex.

## Apex copy rules

Use the approved homepage as the main link. A verified guide shortcut may point to the C-drama field guide. Keep C-drama as an initial focus within Fandom Vibes. Warn visitors to check each reading’s spoiler boundary; never promise that the entire destination is spoiler-free. Do not embed article plot summaries, ending details, or private collections on the apex.
